# Madrasa ERP commercial offer draft

> Status: **UNAPPROVED commercial draft**. This file prepares `pricing_approved` and `messaging_packaging_approved`; it does not satisfy either gate.

## Positioning

A practical Madrasa ERP for institutions that need admissions, student management, academics, attendance, Quran/Hifz, exams/results, documents, portals and daily operations in one system.

## Proposed launch pricing — owner approval required

Proposed annual option: pay for 10 months, receive 12 months.

Proposed first-pilot incentive: waive onboarding/data-import fee for the first 3 approved pilot institutions.

Taxes, payment-gateway charges, WhatsApp/provider usage and hardware/vendor costs are additional where applicable until the tax/provider boundaries are formally approved.

## Explicit launch limitations

These must remain visible in proposals and demos until the underlying boundaries change:

- AI Assistant: **Preview only**; no production AI provider/model.
- Fees/Accounting: **deferred**.
- Payroll: **deferred**; HR core is live.
- Donation/fund accounting: **deferred with Accounting**.
- WhatsApp: runtime exists; real outbound delivery needs approved provider credentials, sender and templates.
- Biometrics/NFC: ERP-side bridge exists; physical deployment requires selected hardware/vendor.
- GPS/live vehicle tracking: **not proven**.
- Urdu/Arabic generated PDFs: full parity is incomplete.
- Razorpay: governed webhook runtime exists; real provider verification remains required before production subscription billing is called verified.
- Recovery: engineering automation exists; real DB + applicable Storage/application recovery evidence remains required.

## Proposed implementation boundary

Included for the launch offer:
- initial tenant setup;
- agreed configuration;
- one structured data-import/onboarding pass within the supported import format;
- administrator orientation;
- reasonable launch support for the agreed modules.

Separate/quoted work:
- custom integrations outside the supported runtime;
- physical biometric/NFC hardware and vendor installation;
- WhatsApp provider/message charges;
- payment-gateway charges;
- bespoke reports/workflows outside the agreed product scope;
- major data cleanup or custom migration outside the supported import path.

## Claims we must not make yet

Do not claim:
- live AI automation;
- live GPS tracking;
- production biometric hardware integration before vendor/device verification;
- production WhatsApp delivery before provider verification;
- production Razorpay billing before provider verification;
- fully complete Urdu/Arabic PDF parity;
- full Fees/Accounting, payroll or donation accounting;
- a passed recovery drill until real evidence exists;
- tax/GST compliance beyond the approved seller/tax boundary.
